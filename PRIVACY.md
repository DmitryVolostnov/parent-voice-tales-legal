# Privacy Policy — Parent Voice Tales

_Last updated: September 14, 2026_

Parent Voice Tales ("the App", "we") helps parents narrate bedtime stories in
their own voice. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## What we collect

**Voice recordings.** When you record your voice, the recording is uploaded to
our server and passed to our speech provider, ElevenLabs, solely to create your
private voice clone. Voice recordings are biometric-like data: we treat them
with strict confidentiality. Your voice clone is used only to narrate stories
inside the App at your request, and is never shared with other users or third
parties beyond the speech provider.

**Story details you provide.** If you use the "Create your own tale" feature,
the details you type or dictate (for example, your child's first name, age, or
favorite toy) are sent to our AI providers solely to create your story: audio
dictation is transcribed by OpenAI (Whisper), the story text is written by
Anthropic (Claude), and illustrations are generated by OpenAI (image models).
The App asks for your explicit permission in-app before the first time any of
this data is sent. Do not include information you don't want processed for
this purpose.

**Story text for narration.** When you narrate a story, its text is sent to
ElevenLabs to synthesize the audio in your voice.

**Generated audio and stories.** Narrated audio files are stored on our
servers (Cloudflare R2) so you can re-listen for free, and cached on your
device.

**Account data.** The App uses an anonymous installation identifier (a random
UUID). We do not collect your name, email, or contacts. Purchase receipts are
validated with Apple to credit tokens.

**We do not collect** analytics profiles, advertising identifiers, precise
location, or your child's personal data. The App is operated by the parent;
children are listeners.

## Consent for voice cloning

You may only clone **your own voice**. Creating a voice requires an explicit
in-app consent confirmation. Recording another person's voice without their
consent violates our Terms of Use and applicable law.

## Data sharing

We share data only with the processors required to run the App:

- **ElevenLabs** — voice cloning and speech synthesis (receives your voice
  recording and the text of stories you narrate)
- **OpenAI** — dictation transcription (Whisper) and story illustrations
  (receives your dictated audio and story details/text)
- **Anthropic** — AI story generation (Claude) (receives the story details you
  provide)
- **Cloudflare** — hosting, storage
- **Apple** — in-app purchase processing

These providers act as our data processors: they process your data only to
provide the features described above, under agreements and privacy policies
that offer the same or equivalent protection as this policy, and they do not
use your data to train AI models. We never sell your data.

## Retention and deletion

Voice clones and audio remain stored while you use the App. Deleting a voice
in Settings removes the voice clone from ElevenLabs as well. You can delete
everything at once with "Delete all my data" in Settings — this removes your
voice clones, generated audio, and balance from our servers immediately. You
can also contact us at the email below with your User ID (Settings) — we will
delete your data within 30 days.

## Children's privacy

The App is intended for use by parents and guardians (17+). We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. Story playback for
children does not require any input from the child.

## Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected in the App and
on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
