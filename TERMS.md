# Terms of Use — Bedtime Stories in Your Voice

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

By using Bedtime Stories in Your Voice ("the App") you agree to these terms.

## 1. The service

The App lets you create a private clone of your own voice and use it to
narrate bedtime stories: from our library, imported by you, or generated by AI
based on your ideas. Narration is powered by third-party AI providers.

## 2. Voice cloning rules

- You may clone **only your own voice**, and only after giving explicit
  consent in the App.
- Cloning or attempting to clone another person's voice without their written
  consent is prohibited and grounds for immediate account termination.
- Your voice clone is private to your account and is never made available to
  other users.
- A "voice from afar" link may only be sent to the person whose voice will be
  recorded; they must record themselves and give their own consent on the
  recording page.

## 3. Tokens and purchases

- Narration is paid for with tokens (1 token ≈ 1,000 characters of text).
- Tokens are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Prices are shown
  before purchase.
- The token cost of a story is always shown before narration. Tokens are
  deducted **only after** the audio is successfully created. If generation
  fails, you are not charged.
- Re-listening to already narrated stories is free.
- Tokens have no monetary value, are non-transferable, and are not redeemable
  for cash. Refunds for purchases are handled by Apple under their policies;
  refunded purchases may result in token balance adjustment.
